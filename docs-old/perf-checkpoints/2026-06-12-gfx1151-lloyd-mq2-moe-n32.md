# gfx1151 MQ2-Lloyd grouped MoE n32 sweep

Date: 2026-06-12
Arch: gfx1151
Command:

```bash
cargo run --release -p rdna-compute --example bench_mq2g256_lloyd_moe_4w \
  2>&1 | tee /tmp/gfx1151-lloyd-mq2-moe-valid-variants-20260612.log
```

The benchmark compares the single-warp MQ2-Lloyd grouped MoE WMMA kernel against
the already-wired gfx1151 grouped variants on synthetic routed-MoE shapes.

Correctness:

- `4w64x16`, `4w64x32`, `4w64x16_cnd`, and `8w128x16` were bit-identical to
  `1w16x16` on all six shapes.
- `4w64x16_mmqload` and `4w64x16_mmqload_nosync` failed badly on every shape
  (`bad` cells were effectively the whole output tensor), so their apparent
  1.6-1.8x speedups are invalid.

Valid-variant performance:

| Shape | 1w16x16 | 4w64x16 | 4w64x32 | 4w64x16_cnd | 8w128x16 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| gate/up B=128 M=2048 K=4096 slots=768 | 1713.6us | 0.90x | 0.92x | 0.83x | 0.88x |
| gate/up B=256 M=2048 K=4096 slots=1536 | 3734.6us | 0.96x | 1.05x | 0.88x | 0.95x |
| gate/up B=1024 M=2048 K=4096 slots=6144 | 15504.1us | 0.98x | 1.09x | 0.91x | 0.99x |
| down B=128 M=4096 K=2048 slots=768 | 1917.4us | 0.96x | 1.11x | 0.88x | 0.96x |
| down B=256 M=4096 K=2048 slots=1536 | 3710.0us | 0.95x | 1.07x | 0.85x | 0.93x |
| down B=1024 M=4096 K=2048 slots=6144 | 15327.1us | 0.98x | 1.11x | 0.93x | 1.01x |

Decision:

- Route Qwen35 MQ2-Lloyd grouped prefill to `4w64x32` only on gfx1151 when
  actual routed slots are at least 1024.
- Keep `1w16x16` for smaller routed-slot counts because B=128 gate/up lost on
  the n32 variant.
- Add `HIPFIRE_MOE_MQ2L_N32_GFX1151=0` as a kill switch and `=1` as a force
  mode for experiments.
