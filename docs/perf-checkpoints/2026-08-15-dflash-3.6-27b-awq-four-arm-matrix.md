## Three findings

**1 — Greedy buys ~17% τ, and it is free.** 2.08 → 2.44. AR decode is
*identical* under both samplings (35.7 → 33.1 either way), so sampling costs
the target nothing; the acceptance difference is entirely draft-vs-target
agreement being harder against a sampled token at `temp 1.0 / top_p 0.95`.
Crossover moves from ctx ≈ 2.5k to ≈ 5k.

**2 — The prefix cache costs DFlash decode throughput, and not via τ.**
Disabling it leaves acceptance untouched (2.44 → 2.47, +1.2%) while decode
rises 11.4% on average and DFlash goes from beating AR on 3/8 turns to 5/8.
**t6 is the isolate: same turn, same context, τ 2.50 → 2.66 — decode
10.5 → 40.3 tok/s, +284%.** Something in the cache-restore path degrades the
DFlash decode loop while leaving speculation quality alone. That is a defect
worth chasing on its own; it is independent of, and additive with, the
drafter-cost story below.

**Disabling the cache is a diagnostic, not a configuration.** Prefill without
it is ruinous — t8 goes 170 ms → 25,496 ms. The decode gain is ~4 s on that
turn against ~25 s of added prefill. Net wall-clock is far worse. The finding
is *where the defect is*, not a recommended setting.

**3 — None of it reaches the τ 4.4–6.1 in `dflash_spec.rs:142`.** Best arm mean
is 2.47, still ~1.8× short of that range's floor. Sampling and prefix caching
are now both eliminated as explanations. What remains untested:

- **draft/target distribution mismatch** — AGENTS.md records the 3.6 drafts as
  trained on 3.5 traces. A matched 3.5 pair is the direct test.
- **card** — that figure predates R9700; it was taken on 7900 XTX.
- **drift** — DFlash logic may have changed since the comment was written.

## Standing conclusion on drafter cost

Unchanged by these arms and reinforced by them: **acceptance is not the failing
variable.** Under every arm the highest-τ turns still lose to AR (greedy t7:
τ = 3.37, 27.7 vs 33.4 tok/s). AR is flat in context under all conditions.
Improving draft quality cannot close this; reducing draft cost per cycle is the
only lever that addresses the measured shape.

## Reproduce

```bash
# arm 3 (cache off) — the diagnostic
HIPFIRE_QWEN_PROMPT_CACHE=0 python3 scripts/serve_harness.py \
  --model ~/.hipfire/models/qwen3-27b-3.6.mq4-awq.remote-mi300x \
  --draft ~/.hipfire/models/qwen36-27b-dflash-mq4.hfq \
  --mode session --thinking-effort medium --sampling greedy \
  --max-seq 32768 --max-tokens 4096 --dflash on
```
