- Date: 2026-08-06
- Branch: `ds4-beta-staging`
- Product commit: `8b690b612` (`perf(deepseek4): eliminate gfx1201 indexer cliff`)
- Parity-probe commit: `c0e193c5e` (`bench(gfx1201): admit DS4 indexer parity probe`)

## Outcome

The 4-R9700 DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 MQ2R route no longer falls into the legacy
single-workgroup O(N²) indexer top-K kernel after the compressed cache reaches
513 rows. On the matched 2,048-prompt / 512-generation fixture, ordinary AR
decode rose from 4.3377 tok/s to 32.2410 tok/s, a 7.4328x recovery. A second
candidate process measured 32.3479 tok/s. The two candidate samples span 0.33%;
the two serial controls measured 4.3370 and 4.3377 tok/s and span 0.015%.

This is a decode-cliff fix, not a prefill optimization. Prefill remained about
61.9-62.2 seconds, or roughly 33.1 tok/s, because the current EP prefill path is
still token-serial.

## Root cause

DeepSeek4 compresses the indexer cache at a 4:1 ratio. The 2,052-token product
fixture therefore crosses from 512 to 513 compressed rows. Before this change,
exact gfx1201 was not admitted to either promoted indexer route and
`indexer_forward` fell through to `indexer_top_k_buf`, whose legacy kernel uses
one workgroup and O(N²) rank counting.

The corrected route is deliberately architecture-local:

- `crates/hipfire-arch-deepseek4/src/forward.rs:497` admits exact gfx1201 and
  defaults the long-capacity two-stage route on.
- `crates/hipfire-arch-deepseek4/src/forward.rs:3182` selects the two-stage
  workspace only when capacity makes it worthwhile.
- `crates/hipfire-arch-deepseek4/src/forward.rs:3380` marks only gfx1151 and
  gfx1201 as eligible for the fast one-launch buffer route.
- `crates/hipfire-arch-deepseek4/src/forward.rs:3398` preserves the initial
  2,048-row capacity bucket on the one-launch route and switches after automatic
  growth.
- `crates/rdna-compute/src/attention.rs:9908` maps exact gfx1201 to the
  wave-size-independent bounded network compiled into a gfx1201 code object.

No enable environment variable is required. The good behavior is the default.
`HIPFIRE_DEEPSEEK4_INDEXER_TOPK_SERIAL=1` was used only to produce the forensic
control; the two-stage `=0` switch remains an emergency diagnostic opt-out and
does not restore the O(N²) initial-bucket path.

## Hardware micro-screen

The repo-native `test_indexer_top_k_buf` probe ran on one R9700 and compared
SERIAL, PARALLEL, BOUNDED, and TWOSTAGE over 13 finite and non-finite cases.
All arms produced identical ordered raw-i32 output to the incumbent eligibility
reference; every finite case also matched the host oracle.

Representative HIP-event results:

| Active compressed rows | Serial | Bounded | Two-stage |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 512 | 0.006 ms | 0.005 ms | 0.032 ms |
| 513 | 8.320 ms | 0.026 ms | 0.029 ms |
| 2,048 | 37.997 ms | 0.051 ms | 0.029 ms |
| 262,144 | 7,282.377 ms | 6.050 ms | 0.154 ms |

The 262,144-row case corresponds to the indexer scale needed at approximately
one million raw tokens. It is mechanism and exactness evidence, not a real
one-million-token serving claim.
