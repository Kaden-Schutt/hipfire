## Result

MQ2RXT is lowerable to a correct retained-PM4 route on gfx1151. The first
route exposed one uncaptured raw HIP launch per transformer layer. After that
launch was migrated to `launch_maybe_blob`, the fresh tape passed 15-position
logits, KV-cache, and recurrent-state parity and produced byte-identical output
on the committed short code fixture.

The matched one-fresh-process product screen was:

| Arm | Decode | Output MD5 |
|---|---:|---|
| Explicit ordinary HIP | 28.0702 tok/s | `a1149e82dd99208082c9de734efc7254` |
| Explicit retained PM4 | 29.0052 tok/s | `a1149e82dd99208082c9de734efc7254` |
| PM4 / HIP | **1.03331x (+3.331%)** | byte-identical |

This is a diagnostic screen (`n=1` fresh process per arm), not a stationary
acceptance median. It establishes that the new route works and has positive
transport headroom; it does not yet authorize automatic `.mq2rxt` admission.

The subsequent 2,048/512 screen and full-tape profile closed the immediate
optimization line. MQ2RXT is now **parked as an experimental, reproducible
SKU**. MQ2R remains the selected product-performance route; no defaults,
registry admission, or model aliases move to MQ2RXT.

Strategically, this first untuned MQ2RXT artifact has already reached and
slightly exceeded the recertified MQ2R retained-PM4 golden line (28.8678
tok/s on the same short-code regime). MQ2RXT PM4 is +0.1374 tok/s (+0.48%)
over that line before any MQ4-specific kernel or route tuning. It therefore
one-shots the throughput reached by the multi-week MQ2R dense-E8SOA campaign;
the E8SOA result is a comparison baseline, not a ceiling or a reason to assume
the MQ4-dense construction has less headroom.

That short-context result did not transfer to the target-depth fixture. The
later evidence below supersedes it for the park/continue decision.

## Target-depth closeout

The repository-native synthetic PP/TG matrix was run at 2,048 prompt tokens,
512 generated tokens, batch 1, greedy sampling, six experts per token, Q8 KV
request mode, thinking off, and speculation off. Each arm was a separate fresh
process with one warmup sequence and three measured rows.

| Arm | Decode median | Samples | Prefill median |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| HIP + hipGraph | 24.6819 tok/s | 24.6811, 24.6819, 24.6840 | 186.2 tok/s |
| Retained PM4 | 27.2818 tok/s | 27.2818, 27.2805, 27.2831 | 185.9 tok/s |

PM4 was 1.1053x HIP on the MQ2RXT route. The retained arm reproduced the
2,320-launch, 32-symbol, `ec2ff8a618b74f30` route with 32/32 contracts and
zero fallbacks.

The preserved MQ2R target-depth result was not remeasured: its already-recorded
two-stage line is 27.7206 tok/s. MQ2RXT is 1.58% below that line. From 27.2818,
30 tok/s requires a 9.96% throughput gain, or approximately 3.32 ms/token.

This matrix is a stable three-row target-depth diagnostic, not a multi-process
ABBA promotion claim. It is sufficient for the requested profile-and-park
decision because the candidate did not beat the preserved product line.

## Occurrence-weighted PM4 profile

A complete steady-state cumulative-prefix sweep covered all 2,320 dispatches.
The full tape measured 35,300.631 us, implying 28.328 device tok/s. The product
PM4 median was 27.2818 tok/s, leaving approximately 1.35 ms/token outside the
profiled device tape rather than enough transport-only headroom to reach 30.

The requested MQ4 families accounted for:

| Family | Launches/token | Packed bytes/token | Attributed time | Effective rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dense `gemv_hfq4g256_multirow_r2` | 511 | 2.815 GB | 12.972 ms | 217 GB/s |
| Grouped `wo_per_group_batched_hfq4g256` | 43 | 0.767 GB | 2.725 ms | 281 GB/s |
| Combined dense MQ4 | 554 | 3.581 GB | 15.697 ms | 228 GB/s |

The packed-byte total comes from the artifact's 6,741,557,248 qt=13 elements
at 136 bytes per 256 weights. Cumulative-prefix differencing has noisy
per-dispatch marginals, including negative samples, so these rates are sizing
evidence rather than standalone kernel benchmarks. The aggregate nevertheless
shows the intended families operating at or above the practical DRAM line once
cache contribution is included. A same-work microkernel rewrite therefore has
no credible two-percent end-to-end projection.

The previously measured same-input projection-pair family also does not reopen
the line: its all-pair E8 analogue projected only 1.52% and delivered 0.25% in
the retained full-model route. MQ2RXT would need removal of material graph work,
not another row-tile, row-pair, X-reuse, or scheduling microvariant.

## Final disposition

- Preserve the MQ2RXT trunk, rejected sidecar, builder, frozen recipe, and raw
  evidence.
- Keep `.mq2rxt` fail-closed: no registry default and no automatic PM4
  admission.
- Do not continue dense MQ4 kernel tuning without a newly measured graph-level
  lever projecting at least 2% end to end.
- Continue product performance work from the certified MQ2R and MQ2R DSpark
  artifacts instead.
- Make no MQ2RXT quality claim from this closeout; no KLD/PPL comparison was
  performed.

## Failure and root cause

Before `29e210a0f`, the captured route was stable but incomplete:

- 2,277 launches
- 31 unique symbols
- sequence hash `e44a32364a36c5a5`
- 31/31 AQL contracts, zero fallbacks
- ordinary retained AQL and retained PM4 both failed parity
- captured-HIP blob, AQL, and PM4 agreed with each other but disagreed with
  the ordinary-HIP forward
- conservative PM4 waits/acquires failed identically, ruling out the lean
  resource wait policy

The exact sequence diff against the passing MQ2R tape showed 43 missing
launches. MQ2RXT uses `wo_per_group_batched_hfq4g256` once per transformer
layer for grouped O-LoRA `wo_a`. That helper still called
`self.hip.launch_kernel` directly, so Redline never recorded it. Ordinary HIP
therefore remained coherent while every retained transport replayed a tape
missing one material projection per layer.

Commit `29e210a0f` migrates only that helper to `launch_maybe_blob` with an
explicit seven-field kernarg blob. No kernel arithmetic, weight, format,
Qwen path, replay policy, or gfx1100 policy changed.

Automatic `.mq2rxt` retained-PM4 admission remains off. The product PM4 arm

retained replay. A repeated/stationary promotion run and the normal serve
battery remain required before changing the product default.
