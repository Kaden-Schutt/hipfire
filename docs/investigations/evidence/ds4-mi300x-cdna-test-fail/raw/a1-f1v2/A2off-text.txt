### 1. The Primacy of Prompt Bytes Over "Text"

The benchmark contract begins with the **exact byte sequence** of the prompt. This is non-negotable for reproducibility. The following must be frozen and verified:

- **Source Corpus**: A deterministic, non-degenerate text (e.g., a technical discussion or prose) with a known MD5/SHA-256 hash. No random words, repeated paragraphs, or timestamps.
- **Tokenization**: The tokenizer must be the exact runtime tokenizer (e.g., `Token::from_hfq_metadata`), not a Python proxy. The tokenizer must produce a **verified round-trip**: encode → decode → re-encode must yield the exact same token IDs and count. This ensures no hidden biases from pre-tokenization regexes, byte fallbacks, or BOS policies.
