**1. The Frozen Prompt Fixture: Why "Bytes" Over "Text"**

The measurement contract begins with a deterministic, versioned prompt fixture. The reason "bytes" matter over "text" is that any variation in the source text (e.g., trailing newlines, character encoding, or tokenizer-specific pre-processing) will produce different token IDs. This invalidates any comparison between runs. The contract therefore mandates:

- **Source Corpus:** A specific, revisioned piece of prose or chat text (not random words or repetitive text). This ensures a realistic mixture of tokens and attention patterns.
- **Tokenization:** The exact tokenizer (from the model's metadata, not a Python proxy) must be used to convert the source corpus to token IDs.
- **Verification:** A round-trip check (decode then re-encode) must produce the exact same sequence of IDs. This ensures the tokenizer is correctly handling the text.
