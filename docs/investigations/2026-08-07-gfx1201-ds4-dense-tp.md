## Mechanism

Each gfx1201 rank loads exact encoded-byte shards of the shared expert:

- `w1` and `w3`: output-row shards.
- `w2`: input-column shards aligned to independent 256-wide FWHT groups.
- No weight conversion or requantization.
- Each local shared partial is added to the rank's already-zeroed routed
  partial before the existing EP allreduce. No additional collective is
  introduced.

All four daemon ranks logged the exact gfx1201 shared-expert TP route.

each rank, and column-shards `wo_b` on whole 256-wide FWHT groups. Each rank
computes its local attention heads and O-LoRA groups, writes a hidden-width
partial, and participates in one RCCL allreduce per attention layer before the

## Peer reduction fused into HC

The first graph-reshape checkpoint replaces both sets of RCCL boundaries with
one fixed-order peer consumer per rank:

- Four process-lifetime release events are re-recorded instead of creating and
  destroying events at every layer.
- Every consumer stream waits on the other three producer events.
- `hc_mix_4stream_peer4_gfx1201` reads all four peer-visible hidden-width
  partials and performs their fixed-order sum inside the existing HC residual
  mix. No all-reduced tensor is materialized.
- Attention and FFN use the same primitive, removing all 86 RCCL groups from
  the admitted route. The FFN path also removes the post-reduce local add.
- Admission requires exact gfx1201, four all-to-all peer-connected ranks,
  MQ2R, attention TP4, and shared-expert TP4. Generic binding defaults remain
  false, so Qwen, MiniMax, gfx1100, gfx1151, other formats, and other rank
  counts keep the prior path.

This is not a retry of the rejected FFN-only peer-add candidate. That attempt
created/destroyed barriers and still materialized the HC input; this checkpoint
extends the seam to attention and consumes the reduction directly.

### Post-RCCL decode timeline

The promoted peer-HC binary was profiled again over the last 32 decode
forwards. The mechanism is confirmed rather than inferred:

- RCCL API and device-kernel domains are absent.
- Dispatches fell by exactly 516/token, from 12,038 to 11,522: 344 RCCL
  device kernels plus 172 reduction/add launches no longer needed at the two
  boundaries per layer.
- Instrumented throughput rose from 22.586 to 23.624 tok/s. This absolute
  number is profiler-perturbed and is not a product claim.
- Per-rank busy time fell to 43.05--43.58% and any-GPU busy time was 63.52%.
  Ranks remain balanced.
- The fused peer-HC consumer is 344 launches/token at 6.010 us average.

The reduction work is gone, but more than eleven thousand token-level
dispatches remain. This makes persistent/coarse graph execution the next
structural lever; it does not support a kernel-bandwidth ceiling claim.
