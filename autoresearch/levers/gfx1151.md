# gfx1151 — Radeon 8060S / Strix Halo (RDNA3.5, iGPU)

Living lever map for gfx1151. See [README.md](README.md) for the method + instrument→lever table.

## Silicon
~40 CU (RDNA3.5), wave32, 2× SIMD32/CU. **Unified LPDDR5X memory (~96 GB usable on hipx), ~256 GB/s** —
the defining constraint: it has *far* less bandwidth than the discrete parts (gfx1100 960, gfx1201 640).
No discrete VRAM; weights stream from the shared LPDDR5X pool. FP8/FP4: RDNA3.5 lacks the RDNA4 fp4 WMMA path.

## Cache behavior: DRAM-THRASHING (the defining measurement)
Daemon-measured `profile_feedback` on a3b mq4r decode: **L2-hit ~16.7%, mem_busy HIGH** → the decode kernels
**miss cache and stream from LPDDR5X.** This is the *opposite* of gfx1201 (L2-resident 64-78%) and the *same
class* as gfx1100 (DRAM-bound). Consequence: gfx1151's bottleneck is **DRAM traffic**, and the levers that work
here are the gfx11 family (traffic reduction), NOT the gfx12 family (cache/ALU/occupancy).

## THE primary lever: TRAFFIC REDUCTION (read fewer TOTAL bytes)
Because L2-hit is 16.7%, the win is cutting the *byte-count* streamed from LPDDR5X, not fetching the same bytes
faster:
- **Bigger row-tiles that reuse the activation X across more output rows** — the gfx11 "traffic-cut" lever.
- **Fewer weight passes** — fuse passes that re-stream the same weights.
- Wider loads (uint2/uint4) are **SECONDARY** — they improve coalescing (how efficiently bytes are fetched),
  NOT how many bytes are fetched. Sub-percent-only on their own (see ledger). Do not stop at load-width.

The Stage-1 BOD prompt (`hip_task_bod.txt`) now encodes this: L2-hit LOW + mem_busy HIGH ⇒ traffic reduction.

## Physics: why gfx1151 is the arch with the most predicted `.hip` headroom
a3b decode is **latency/batch-1-bound**, not raw-BW-bound. So gfx1151's low LPDDR5X bandwidth does NOT fully
explain being <½ of gfx1100 (78.8 vs 175). That gap is where occupancy/latency + traffic-reduction levers live —
unlike gfx1100/gfx1201 where `.hip` micro-tuning is tapped (fresh-process ~0%). gfx1151 is the best current
candidate to produce a **fresh-process-surviving** win. (If traffic-reduction *also* only nets sub-percent here,
that's strong evidence the whole mq4 format — not the kernel — is the ceiling: escalate to format redesign.)

## Baseline
- **a3b mq4r decode: 78.8 tok/s** (HIP device 1 on hipx, q8 KV, standard prompt, fresh-process). This is the
  `loop_baseline_gfx1151` anchor. mfp4e8 not yet measured on this arch.

## Device mapping (hipx) — HIP/ROCR order ≠ rocminfo/DRM order (verified 2026-07-06)
```
HVD=0 → gfx1100   HVD=1 → gfx1151   HVD=2 → gfx1010 (5700XT)   HVD=3 → gfx1030
```
The gfx1151 loop MUST pin `HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=1`. rocminfo lists gfx1151 at index 2 — do NOT trust it; probe
the daemon's reported arch per HVD. Unpinned daemons (`HVD` unset) enumerate ALL GPUs on init and spin the
5700XT harmlessly — always pin HVD.

## Wins / deads on record
- `gate_ptr_iter` (gate_up) +0.654%, `down_uint2_scale` (moe_down) +0.595% — WON **within-session**, but
  **sub-percent and UNVERIFIED**: coalescing-class (uint2 loads / ptr-iter), not traffic reduction. Same shape
  that fresh-process-REJECTED on gfx1100 (−0.34%). Must clear `rollover_stack.sh` before they count.
- `down_uint2_ptr_iter` — DEAD.
- **Open**: traffic-reduction (row-tile / X-reuse) candidates — the primary lever, not yet properly attacked
  (early candidates were all coalescing; the BOD prompt now forces a pivot to traffic after 3 coalescing tries).

## Toolchain gotcha (setup)
Fresh worktrees (`sw_cardN/.swhome`) lack a rustup default → certify `cargo build` fails with "could not choose
a version of cargo" (mis-reads as VARIANT_BUILD_FAIL). Fix: symlink `~/.rustup`/`~/.cargo` into `.swhome`, or
pass `RUSTUP_HOME=~/.rustup CARGO_HOME=~/.cargo` (baked into the gfx1151 prompt). Set `rustup default` globally
to prevent recurrence. Certify is also SLOW on the iGPU (~10 min/candidate) — consider cheap-mode (3 samples).
